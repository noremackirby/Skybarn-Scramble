Controls

  • Red Player: A and D to move, W to jump, S to dash
  • Blue Player: Left and Right to move, Up to jump, Down to dash
  • CPU vs CPU: Green and Purple take turns automatically
  • Drafting: The round winner picks 1 trap card, presses R when rotatable, and places it 2 times. Erase cannot hit fresh traps.
  • Win Condition: Land on the checkered finish platform. Match target is chosen before the game starts.
  • Story Mode: Clear the gauntlets one attempt at a time. Checkpoints save your latest story respawn if the map includes them.

What Makes It Work

Website and game in one page

The page is a normal website layout wrapped around a playable canvas game, so you can open one file in Chrome and get both the presentation and the gameplay.

Original Design

Built from scratch

The visuals, course, scoring, obstacle types, and interaction flow are all original to avoid copying another game's protected content.
